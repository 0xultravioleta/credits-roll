- Record locally before uploading. Use a recoverable recording format such as MKV and remux to MP4 afterward. Check disk space. Make a ten-second test and play it back.
- Set narration peaks around **−12 to −6 dB**, with no clipping. Keep game audio/music below speech. Avoid capturing the stream twice through both browser and desktop audio. Use only music, footage and other material the owner has rights to include.
- Before Record, open the owner's stream and chat, the dashboard, OBS overlay, the actual confirmed clip page, the actual Prediction card and any verified result/refund view. - Hide private overlay URL query strings and dashboard copy fields. Capture overlay content without its address bar. Do not show `.env`, OAuth callback URLs, API keys, cookies, debug downloads or the full private ledger. The public Twitch clip URL can appear only after checking the footage and title are appropriate to share.
- Use an authorized, consenting participant for a new example. Do not expose a forgotten viewer's old evidence. Do not claim local erasure deletes a Twitch-hosted clip or recalls a payout.
- Confirm every live segment against what was actually recorded. Keep a visible source label on every synthetic segment. Do not draw a fake native Twitch Prediction card or manufacture chat notices, refunds, balances, stakes or winnings.
